> **An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, geographic distribution, product performance, and profit margin trends.**

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

This project was created as part of my journey learning **Tableau and Data Analytics**, using the **Superstore dataset** to build an interactive business dashboard.

The objective was to move beyond individual charts and create a dashboard that brings multiple business questions together in one place.

The analysis focuses on:

* 💰 Sales performance
* 📈 Profitability
* 📦 Orders
* 📊 Profit Margin
* 🗺️ Geographic performance
* 🏷️ Category & Sub-Category performance
* 🏆 Top-performing products
* 📅 Sales and profit trends over time

---

## 🧠 Business Objective

The main objective was to answer a simple business question:

> **How is the company performing in terms of sales and profitability, and what factors appear to be driving that performance?**

To answer this, I built several visualizations that allow the user to move from a high-level overview into more detailed analysis.

---

# 📌 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard is organized into **five main analytical sections**.

---

## 1. 💰 Executive KPIs

The dashboard starts with four key performance indicators:

| KPI                  | Description                               |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 💰 **Total Sales**   | Total revenue generated by the company    |
| 📈 **Total Profit**  | Total profit generated                    |
| 📦 **Total Orders**  | Number of orders placed                   |
| 📊 **Profit Margin** | Percentage of sales converted into profit |

### Profit Margin

The profit margin was calculated using:

```text
Profit Margin = Profit / Sales × 100
```

This KPI provides additional context to Sales and Profit.

A company can increase its sales while becoming less profitable, so looking at revenue alone does not provide the complete picture.

---

# 2. 📈 Sales & Profit Over Time

A time-series visualization was created to analyze how **Sales and Profit change over time**.

### Main Question

> **How are sales and profit evolving over time?**

The visualization uses:

* `Order Date` → Time dimension
* `Sales` → Sales measure
* `Profit` → Profit measure

This visualization makes it possible to identify changes in performance and compare revenue growth with profitability.

### Analytical Perspective

One important business scenario to investigate is:

> **Sales increasing while Profit decreases.**

This could indicate that the company is generating more revenue without achieving the same level of profitability.

This type of comparison demonstrates why analyzing multiple metrics together is important.

---

# 3. 🗺️ Sales by State

A geographic visualization was created using **State** and **Sales**.

Sales were represented through color intensity, allowing higher-performing states to stand out visually.

### Main Question

> **Where are we generating the most sales?**

The map also provides an interactive way to explore individual states and investigate their contribution to overall sales performance.

### Key Finding

**California generated the highest Sales and also the highest Profit among the states analyzed.**

This makes California an important contributor to the company's overall financial performance.

---

# 4. 📦 Category & Sub-Category Analysis

The dashboard includes category-level and sub-category-level analysis.

### Sales by Category

The main product categories analyzed were:

* 🪑 Furniture
* 💻 Technology
* 📎 Office Supplies

The analysis identified:

> **Furniture as the category generating the highest Sales.**

### Profit by Sub-Category

A second visualization was created to compare profitability across individual sub-categories.

The sub-categories were sorted from highest to lowest Profit to make performance differences easier to identify.

### Key Finding

> **Tables generated the lowest Profit among the sub-categories analyzed.**

This highlights an important analytical distinction:

**High sales volume does not necessarily mean high profitability.**

---

# 5. 🏆 Top 10 Products by Sales

The final section focuses on the products generating the highest sales.

A **Top N filter** was used to identify the 10 products with the highest Sales.

### Main Question

> **Which products contribute the most to total sales?**

This visualization also provided practical experience with one of Tableau's important filtering capabilities:

### `Top N Filtering`

Instead of displaying every product, the visualization dynamically focuses on the **Top 10 products by Sales**.

---

# 🔎 Business Questions & Findings

After exploring the dashboard, I used it to answer several business questions.

| Business Question                                                                   | Finding                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Which category generates the most Sales?                                            | **Furniture**                                                      |
| Which category generates the most Profit?                                           | **Technology**                                                     |
| Which Sub-Category generates the least Profit?                                      | **Tables**                                                         |
| Which region/state has the highest Sales?                                           | **California**                                                     |
| Which region/state has the highest Profit?                                          | **California**                                                     |
| Is the Top 10 Sales product group the same as the group generating the most Profit? | **No**                                                             |
| What happened to Profit over the analyzed period?                                   | Profit increased from approximately **51K to 95K**                 |
| What happened to Profit Margin?                                                     | **Profit Margin declined despite the increase in absolute Profit** |

---

# 💡 Key Insights

### 1. Revenue and profitability tell different stories

Furniture generated the highest Sales, while Technology generated the highest Profit.

This demonstrates that the category generating the most revenue is not necessarily the category generating the most profit.

---

### 2. High-selling products are not necessarily the most profitable

The Top 10 products by Sales were not identical to the products or groups contributing most to Profit.

This reinforces the importance of analyzing **profitability alongside revenue**.

---

### 3. California is a major contributor

California stood out as the strongest state in terms of both Sales and Profit.

This indicates that geographic performance can play an important role in understanding the company's overall results.

---

### 4. Profit increased while Profit Margin declined

Profit increased from approximately **51K to 95K** over the analyzed period.

However, the Profit Margin declined.

This is an important distinction:

> **The company generated more absolute profit, but profit represented a smaller proportion of revenue.**

This suggests that revenue growth alone does not necessarily mean that the business is becoming more efficient or profitable.
